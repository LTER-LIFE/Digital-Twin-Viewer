## Colour map design references

The six hand-authored colour maps in `js/colormaps.js` are short lists of
interpolation stops written for this application. No files from the following
projects are included; they are credited as the designs the stops follow.

- `thermal`, `haline`, `algae`, `turbid`: after cmocean (Thyng, K. M., Greene,
  C. A., Hetland, R. D., Zimmerle, H. M. and DiMarco, S. F., 2016, True colors of
  oceanography, *Oceanography* 29(3), 9-13, <https://doi.org/10.5670/oceanog.2016.66>).
  cmocean is released under the MIT License, Copyright (c) 2015 Kristen M. Thyng.
- `viridis`: after the matplotlib colour map by Stéfan van der Walt and
  Nathaniel Smith, released under CC0 1.0.
